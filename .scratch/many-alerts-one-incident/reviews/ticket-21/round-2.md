# Ticket 21 — second decision round accepted

The human answered “Agree” to all five second-round recommendations after accepting all five first-round decisions. These choices finish the recovery contract without changing ticket 14's Match rules or ticket 31's latest-admitted dedupe baseline.

1. Budget. Keep the standing five-minute total Run budget: 270 seconds for startup/work, then up to 20 seconds for interruption/flush, then 10 seconds for forced termination/reaping, all measured from Run launch with a monotonic clock. Operator cancellation enters cleanup sooner and cannot extend the original deadline. Queue wait is separately visible. If containment/reaping is not confirmed at the bound, report containment failure and hold dispatch rather than falsely claim the processes are gone. The historical 370-second Run will not fit; model/Report selection must fit the budget rather than silently extend it. No live performance is promised. SIGINT is a best-effort interruption request, not a guarantee of final usage/result data.
2. Recovery persistence. Add a Receiver-owned durable recovery journal, explicitly separate in purpose and access from the Run-written Memory directory. Record admitted work identity, pending/held source records and arrival order, dedupe baseline, execution outcome, mutation intents before dispatch and observed confirmations/uncertainties, plus references needed for reconciliation. This is a new persistence exception to ADR 0005, not a second authority for Incident state; fresh OPS reads remain authoritative. Runs cannot alter the journal. Preserve it across pod/container restarts within a rehearsal; a restart begins with dispatch held and old sentinels invalid. Failure to durably admit work must not be acknowledged as successful admission. No credentials, raw prompt/tool bodies or Ground truth belong there. Unresolved work requires explicit disposition before reset; persistence after cluster destruction remains outside the guarantee.
3. Failed/pending merge. On authorized retry, reconcile external effects first, then derive fresh work from outstanding failed Alerts plus newer pending Alerts. Use Receiver admission order to choose the newest status/values for each Fingerprint and retain source-group provenance; omission never means Resolved. Already confirmed operations are not reissued, but a newer Alert state may justify a new operation after fresh Match evaluation. Exact repeats stay suppressed against latest-admitted state while the outstanding-work journal remains eligible for explicit retry. A retry never replays a captured command list or blindly writes an old Report. Apply ticket 14's eligibility window at retry time; aging out does not erase an unresolved operation's reconciliation obligation.
4. Terminal evidence. Success requires a recognized, well-formed terminal result with an explicit non-error indicator and success subtype, a clean process exit, no overriding Receiver timeout/cancel/containment error, and separately accounted required effects or justified no-op outcomes. Result-level is_error, error terminal reason, or nonzero exit prevents success; missing required fields, conflicting/duplicate terminal results or malformed evidence are incomplete. Keep diagnostics showing both reported and Receiver-derived outcomes. Usage may be absent even when other evidence is sufficient; display unknown rather than invent zero. Operation confirmation must come from trusted Forwarder responses/read-back evidence, not the model's prose alone.
5. Operator actions. Expose explicit cancel, inspect/reconcile, retry and resume controls owned by the operator, not callable by Runs. Retry launches at most one fresh attempt after reconciliation, with a fresh Run ID, sentinels and the same time budget; repeated failure holds dispatch again. Resume releases ordinary dispatch only after held work is completed or explicitly disposed of and required boundaries are ready. Preserve a record of any human decision to abandon unprocessed work; do not let abandonment silently settle unknown external writes. No automatic model fallback, automatic mutation replay or unlimited retry loop. Optional Memory repairs target only the missing step; confirmed OPS work is not rerun to regenerate a draft or learning entry.
