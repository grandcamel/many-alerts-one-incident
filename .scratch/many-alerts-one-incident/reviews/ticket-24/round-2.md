# Ticket 24 — second decision round accepted

The human accepted all five recommendations with “Agree.” Round 1 is also accepted. These choices complete the policy; exact schemas, collector design and offline fixtures will graduate to a specification ticket rather than imply an implemented scorer.

1. Lifecycle rollup. Early Reports may explicitly leave the Mechanism undetermined or partial while investigation proceeds; that alone does not fail a sample. Every asserted observation or causal claim still needs supporting retrieved evidence, and uncertainty must remain visible. Require a correct supported final Mechanism, supported claims throughout, corrected arithmetic in the final Report, and no invented observations or confidently wrong causal assertions in an earlier Report for a clean diagnostic lifecycle pass. Preserve any earlier arithmetic defect separately: a corrected Report may pass as a revision, but an originally defective lifecycle does not become a clean qualification sample. Do not average grades into a numeric score that hides a hard failure. No final Report, gaps preventing audit or unfinished review remain incomplete/unverifiable, never a pass. ADRs 0012/0013 separately gate execution, effects, time and spend; a diagnostic pass alone is not model qualification.
2. Reviewer and disputes. One named human reviewer may adjudicate an ordinary sample before it counts toward qualification or a published rehearsal trend. For a disputed causal or evidence judgment, require a second named human review; until reconciled, mark disputed and exclude it from qualifying passes. Retain both rationales and append a dated superseding adjudication instead of overwriting a verdict. Separate correction of the Report from correction of a reviewer mistake. No new model judge or paid review runs. On stage, show pending until the human review exists, not a machine-produced pass.
3. Evidence retention and capture failure. Store private local audit evidence outside Run access and outside Git, limited to 100 MiB per Run and 2 GiB total, retained for at most 30 days. Keep compact adjudication records and artifact digests locally in the planning repository; digests establish identity, not claim support after evidence expires. Preflight a qualification attempt for capture readiness and capacity. If capture fails or exceeds bounds during Incident work, preserve safe evidence plus an explicit gap and mark affected claims/sample unverifiable; do not block required OPS handling, grow storage without bound or silently evict unreviewed evidence. At expiry delete raw audit evidence and label the surviving verdict historically reviewed but no longer independently re-auditable from that bundle. Record exclusions/loss explicitly, never serialize credentials or account identity. No upload or publication is authorized.
4. Qualification coverage. Use the three accepted lifecycle samples per candidate as two primary-presentation Fault samples and one designated-fallback Fault sample. Require at least one cold-start and one Memory-assisted sample, recording which is which and preserving actual model/auth/venue configuration. These are labelled coverage observations, not enough samples to claim a controlled causal comparison or success rate. Qualify only the demonstrated presentation/fallback scope, not the whole Fault menu. Any failed or unverifiable sample prevents that three-sample set from qualifying; replacement clean samples require the existing weekly allocations or later weeks, and failures remain in the record. Before using a Fault in qualification, its repository-only Mechanism/Trigger ground truth must exist and be reviewed; additional Fault definitions graduate to their own bounded work. No paid samples are run by accepting this plan.
5. Frozen record and rubric changes. Use versioned JSON per Report revision and one linked lifecycle manifest, with readable summaries generated from those records. Minimum fields: artifact/revision identities and digests, Fault and Run/Incident linkage, rubric version, model/auth/venue/Memory condition, reviewer/time, claim-to-retrieval mapping, observed versus inferred status, query/time scope and completeness, mechanism/evidence/arithmetic grades, execution/effect/time/spend references, defects and rationale. Freeze the rubric before a comparison set. A rubric change starts a new series unless all retained evidence is explicitly re-adjudicated under it; retain the old verdicts. Never retroactively convert the old flag-name prototype grades into current Mechanism qualification.

After acceptance, create the ADR and a specification follow-up for bounded evidence capture, access/retention, schemas/rollups, mechanical checks and offline fixtures. Integrate with tickets 16, 34, 35 and 38 without putting scoring material into Run-readable stores. No runtime, Skill or paid execution changes.
