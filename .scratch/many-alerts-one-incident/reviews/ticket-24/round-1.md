# Ticket 24 — first decision round accepted

The human accepted all five recommendations with “Agree.”

Accepted prerequisites: score the Mechanism, never Trigger-name presence (ADR 0008:5,18); system claims must trace to retrieved system evidence (ADR 0010:17); the shared sanitized feed is insufficient for a full citation audit (ADR 0010:19). Legitimately inferred causes and retrieved Changes may enter Memory, but adjudication Ground truth may not (ADR 0009:27). Ticket 22 requires cited evidence and three complete lifecycle samples for qualification; this round defines scoring, not new paid execution.

1. Adjudicator. Recommend deterministic prechecks for schema, citation/reference linkage, available retrieval provenance and computable arithmetic. A named human reviewer decides causal attribution, whether retrieved responses actually support claims, and the final verdict. Mechanical checks may flag defects but cannot award a semantic pass. No paid model judge and no keyword-based diagnosis grade.
2. Rubric. Recommend separate Mechanism grades (correct / partial / wrong / undetermined) and evidence grades (supported / unsupported / unverifiable), with reviewer rationale and explicit observed-versus-inferred claims. A supported, clearly labelled causal inference may earn a correct Mechanism grade without a directly observed Trigger. An invented observation/control or unsupported causal claim fails evidence and prevents an overall pass even if the Mechanism happens to be correct. Missing evidence records mean unverifiable, not proof of fabrication, and cannot qualify a model. Naming a flag, component or retrieved Change alone earns no Mechanism pass. Execution/effect outcomes, elapsed time and spend remain separate dimensions under ADRs 0012/0013.
3. Audit evidence. Recommend a private operator-owned local evidence bundle outside Run mounts, Memory and shared telemetry. Preserve exact reviewed Report revisions, correlated Run/tool request and returned-response evidence, query/time scope, gaps and source provenance sufficient for claim-by-claim review. Sanitized dashboards, tool names alone, or later re-queries cannot establish what the Run retrieved at the time. Remove credentials/account identity; if redaction or truncation removes necessary support, mark the claim unverifiable. No Ground truth or scoring feedback enters Run-readable stores. The next specification must set capture access, size and retention bounds; approval of this contract does not authorize collecting secrets or unrestricted raw bodies.
4. Review unit and record. Recommend per-Run/per-Report-revision audit records linked into one Fault lifecycle summary. Review every created/updated Report in a qualification sample, retaining failures and corrections rather than replacing them with the final polished Report. Store a versioned local structured record plus readable summary with reviewer, rubric version, Fault/Run/Incident identities, evidence references and verdict rationale. Derive rehearsal trends from those records, keeping Fault/model/auth/venue/rubric and cold-versus-Memory-assisted samples distinct. During presentation show only a sanitized verdict summary labelled pending until reviewed; keep adjudication material out of Run-readable telemetry.
5. Arithmetic. Recommend deterministic checks of durations/counts/rates when cited inputs and units make them reproducible. Require correction of incorrect displayed arithmetic before a clean Report pass, even if the discrepancy does not change the Mechanism. Preserve the original defect and distinguish presentation/arithmetic error from invented evidence or wrong diagnosis. Legitimate rounding is acceptable when precision and units are stated; absent inputs remain unverifiable rather than guessed.

Next dependent frontier: exact schema/rollup and materiality rules, reviewer workflow/disputes, evidence retention/access and capture completeness, qualification/fallback coverage and a bounded offline acceptance specification. All five recommendations are accepted; no scorer or runtime code is changed.
