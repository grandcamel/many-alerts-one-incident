# Ticket 25 — second decision round accepted

The human accepted all five recommendations with “Agree.” Round 1 is also accepted. These are design limits, not observed performance or deployed guarantees. No cluster, credentials, mutation, model or demo execution is authorized.

1. Ordering and identity. Serialize presenter actions across the whole demo, because flag changes share configuration and the flagd rollout. Permit one active action and no queued new injections. Give each request a stable Change ID and immutable requested payload; re-submission with that ID returns recorded progress, and a conflicting payload is rejected. Persist stage/dispatch intent before mutation. Reconcile uncertain writes/rollouts after interruption rather than blindly repeating them. Undo is a distinct linked Change with priority once in-flight state has been reconciled; it must never race an unresolved injection. Unexpected resource-version/value drift holds ordinary action admission. Restart begins held until journal and observed state reconcile. Explicit request/stage sequence orders the record; wall-clock and observation times are separately recorded, without treating ingestion order as causal order.
2. Durability and retention. Keep an operator-only durable in-cluster Change journal, separate from Receiver recovery/Memory, surviving pod/coordinator restarts within the rehearsal. Cap it at 100 MiB and retain Change journal records and the dedicated Loki Change stream for seven days. Reserve 10 MiB of journal capacity for recovery/undo records; ordinary injection cannot consume that reserve. Preserve unresolved actions until reconciled or explicitly handed off to a private operator recovery record before journal expiry/reset/cluster destruction; never silently age away uncertain action state. A destructive reset requires operator disposition/export, not assumed cluster-destruction durability. Capacity, failed durable writes or overdue unresolved retention hold new injections. If even recovery recording is unavailable, preserve a direct operator emergency-undo path with an explicit audit gap; restoring the system takes precedence over a complete record. This is a new bounded persistence requirement, not ADR 0010's best-effort Run-telemetry spool.
3. Deadlines and delivery. Allow at most 180 seconds per coordinated actuation attempt, including config write, rollout and bounded read-back; measure monotonic elapsed time. Allow up to 30 seconds after a stage is queued for its record to become queryable through the approved Eyes path. These are stop/hold thresholds, not guarantees that a timed-out upstream action stopped. A missed deadline records incomplete/unknown state and holds further injections; recovery reconciles actual state first. Symptom and Incident recovery timing remain separate. Retry telemetry delivery from the journal with stable Change/stage IDs, bounded backoff and at most three sends per stage per operator-authorized delivery attempt; duplicates remain identifiable and are deduplicated for display. Exhaustion leaves the stage pending and requires operator inspection/resume, not an infinite loop or repeated actuation. Prove current-rehearsal stream retrieval readiness with a distinctly marked diagnostic record before injection; a delivery receipt alone is insufficient. Optional annotation delivery cannot gate actuation or redefine Change success.
4. Authority and visible identity. Use a separate operator-controlled coordinator identity whose API permissions are scoped to the named demo ConfigMap and necessary named Deployments, plus required read-back resources. Enforce allowed flag/variant fields and permitted rollout actions in the coordinator, not merely broad Kubernetes verbs. Preserve unrelated configuration with fresh version/precondition checks. Runs receive no mutation token, coordinator control access or emergency operator credential. Expose only a stable presenter alias in Change telemetry; record the authenticated calling principal privately where available and never imply the alias alone proves a natural person's identity. No arbitrary shell/exec or caller-selected targets. Disable or restrict the flagd-ui editing route during managed demonstrations; direct administrator intervention is an explicit emergency path, not a second silently supported injection route.
5. Undo and qualification. Undo applies the recorded pre-injection flag value under fresh state checks and performs the already required Fault-specific service restart; no blanket overwrite of concurrent changes. If state has drifted, require the operator to select/reconcile the recovery action. Record requested, accepted and observed stages honestly; an off value does not prove symptoms recovered or Incidents completed. Incident handling stays governed by accepted Alert/member rules. Mark lifecycles with untracked interventions, unresolved stages or material Change-record gaps ineligible for a clean qualification sample, even when emergency recovery succeeds. Keep the failure visible; a repaired record or later rerun does not erase it. A separately labelled replay remains the presentation fallback until the Change path and other qualification gates pass.

After acceptance: write an ADR and a bounded specification follow-up covering schema, exact API/read-back capabilities, mounts/identity/grants, size/expiry enforcement, idempotency/crash/delivery fixtures and intended-venue acceptance. Amend ADR 0005's persistence scope explicitly. Integrate Eyes/Forwarder, venue lifetime, scoring and qualification tickets without reopening accepted rules. No changes to runtime or Skill code.
