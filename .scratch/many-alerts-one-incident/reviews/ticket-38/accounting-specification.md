# Ticket 38 accounting and model-qualification specification

Status: source-only proposed implementation contract, 2026-09-22.

This document defines the accounting and qualification mechanics requested by ticket 38.
It does not implement or invoke a Receiver, Forwarder, provider client, billing API,
model, tenant, or paid Run. The ticket-23 ledger and ticket-36 mediated-client fixture
are offline design references only. They do not establish provider charges, native-client
compatibility, direct-route prevention, account limits, model quality, or venue
acceptance.

## Authority and boundary

**Accepted policy.** [ADR 0013](../../../../docs/adr/0013-demo-spend-is-metered-reserved-and-qualified.md)
settles:

- Provider API billing is actual-spend authority. SDK, Transcript, and list-price values
  are estimates; missing usage is unknown. Cloud and venue cost remain separate.
- The Monday--Sunday America/New_York envelope is $150: three rehearsal Fault
  lifecycles at $30 each, one presentation lifecycle at $30, and $30 diagnostics/retries.
  Each lifecycle allows at most ten attempted Runs, diagnostics at most ten attempts,
  and every attempt reserves $3 before launch.
- A retry consumes diagnostics and its lifecycle limit but charges once in the weekly
  total. Unknown exposure/billing lag holds model dispatch; reset and rollover do not
  settle a charge.
- [ADR 0014](../../../../docs/adr/0014-report-scoring-requires-supported-claims-and-human-review.md)
  requires three representative intended-auth/venue lifecycles: two
  primary-presentation Faults and one designated fallback, with at least one cold and
  one Memory-assisted condition.

Everything marked **Proposed** is a routine design choice for a later implementation.
It is conservative and does not amend the accepted policy. A provider receipt or billing
snapshot below is an authorized future authenticated provider record; no current fixture
is one.

The Receiver is the only accounting writer and admission reader. A Run receives only
opaque `attempt_id` at launch. It cannot read, write, mount, or infer the ledger,
reservation, provider record, account identity, or hold. Records exclude credentials,
sentinels, raw prompts/tool bodies, Ground truth, and audit bodies. They retain only
opaque/pseudonymized identifiers and sanitized evidence digests under
[ADR 0010](../../../../docs/adr/0010-run-telemetry-is-sanitized-correlated-and-best-effort.md).

## Proposed one-store design

Use one Receiver-owned transactional database with separate logical table families:

- the **recovery journal**, for ticket-37 admission, effect, and reconciliation state;
  and
- **accounting**, for reservations, money attribution, billing evidence, configuration,
  and holds.

The purposes and access policy remain distinct, but an admission reservation and the
journal entry commit together. This avoids assuming distributed atomicity among a journal
database, accounting database, and Forwarder. Retention horizons also stay distinct.
A future split-store design is acceptable only with an equivalent crash-safe no-launch
handshake; it must not assume atomic commit with the Forwarder.

Every mutation takes the exclusive Receiver writer transaction, appends its immutable
event, updates the materialized projection, checks capacity, and commits durably before
reporting success. Clean exit, stale snapshot, or restored file is never billing
evidence.

| Entity | Relationship |
| --- | --- |
| `journal_generation` / `admission_id` | Link a reservation to durable admission and current recovery-journal generation. |
| `attempt_id` / `reservation_id` | Receiver-generated and immutable. One reservation per attempt; attempt ID flows to journal, lease, and cost receipts. |
| `run_id` / `lease_id` | Recorded after reservation. Retry uses fresh Run and lease IDs. |
| `operation_id` / `intent_id` | Journal-owned effect correlation; referenced only when relevant to the attempt. |
| source fingerprint/group | Journal provenance showing a hold did not discard admitted source records. |

IDs are lowercase UUIDs and are generated by the Receiver. A retry creates fresh
attempt, reservation, Run, lease, and relevant operation IDs after effect reconciliation.

### Admission and crash protocol

1. Validate candidate, lifecycle/sample slot, readiness, allocation, and source
   references before reserving.
2. In one transaction calculate the week, reject a blocking hold, check allocation,
   lifecycle/diagnostic spend and count limits, bounded liability, experiment cap, and
   capacity, then append `reservation_created` and `admission_reserved` and commit.
   A financial rejection records `model_launch_held` against the already durable
   journal admission; it does not reject, erase, or acknowledge away the Notification.
3. Register the Forwarder lease using committed attempt ID, Run ID, scope, and expiry.
   It is idempotent Receiver-to-Forwarder control; its receipt contains no sentinel or
   upstream credential.
4. Persist `lease_registered`. Only a matching committed reservation and lease receipt
   permit `launch_claimed` to commit. Invoke the launcher after that commit.
5. Dispatch, terminal, billing, and reconciliation observations are later transactions.
   A process result, model prose, estimate, usage, or clean exit does not settle cost.

A crash before step 2 has no admission. A crash after it leaves reservation exposure.
A crash after Forwarder registration before local recording is reconciled against control
records and sets `forwarder_control_unreconciled`. A crash after `launch_claimed` but
before/during spawn is `launch_or_dispatch_unknown`. A crash after dispatch cannot prove
cancellation, zero cost, or no upstream effect.

Receiver or Forwarder restart invalidates old leases and begins with model dispatch held.
Reconcile unsettled attempts and ticket-37 effects before a new launch. Accounting does
not become an Incident database; ticket 37 remains authoritative for effects.

## Exact schema and events

All money is signed integer USD microdollars (`amount_usd_micros`), never float. Positive
is a charge; negative is a documented provider credit/refund. Reject booleans, fractions,
non-finite values, and values outside signed 64-bit range. Every amount requires
`currency: "USD"`. Non-USD sets a currency hold rather than being converted.

Every immutable event has `schema_version`, `event_id`, `recorded_at_utc`, and sanitized
`actor_kind` (receiver, operator, billing_import). Times are RFC 3339 UTC. A week is
calculated at reservation in `America/New_York`, Monday 00:00 inclusive to the next
Monday exclusive. Store boundary instants and IANA timezone; never recalculate old weeks.

| Record/event | Required fields | Invariant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `week_configured` | week key, timezone, weekly cap, allocation/counter/capacity revision | Existing-week configuration is immutable; revisions apply prospectively. |
| `allocation_configured` | allocation ID, week, kind, ceiling, attempt ceiling, approval reference | Kinds are rehearsal lifecycle, presentation lifecycle, diagnostics. Reallocation needs explicit approval. |
| `lifecycle_registered` | lifecycle, Fault, candidate, scope, primary allocation, sample slot | Compact non-Ground-truth context only. |
| `reservation_created` | attempt/reservation, lifecycle or diagnostic purpose, week, financial allocation, retry predecessor, candidate/auth/venue pseudonyms, reservation R, defensible total liability U | R is $3 policy reservation; U is the documented upper exposure used for admission and must be at least R. One financial allocation per attempt. |
| `lease_registered` / `launch_claimed` / `dispatch_observed` / `dispatch_uncertain` / `terminal_observed` | attempt, receipt digest/reference, observation time, result category | Operational evidence only, never cost settlement. |
| `estimate_reported` | attempt, exact optional usage, estimate, source, digest | Informational only; no availability effect. |
| `experiment_cost_committed` / `experiment_cost_line_recorded` | cost class (support, review, venue), commitment U or unique actual line, evidence digest, experiment/week attribution | Never enters model allocation limits; it enters aggregate experiment liability and needs final coverage or a hold. |
| `provider_charge_line_recorded` | provider/account pseudonym, provider charge-line identity, occurrence time, amount/currency, billing digest, attempt, attribution week | A unique line defensibly links to one attempt; several distinct lines may belong to one multi-request attempt. |
| `provider_actual_unknown` / `provider_coverage_completed` | attempt, reason or final coverage period, checked snapshot references/digest | No partial set of lines settles an attempt. Only explicit complete coverage permits actual-sum settlement; missing coverage is a hold. |
| `billing_snapshot_recorded` | source period, fetched time, account pseudonym, digest, completeness/lag state | Establishes only documented coverage. |
| `hold_set` / `hold_cleared` | stable hold ID, scope, reason, triggering events, disposition ref | Sticky until exact reason is reconciled. |
| `qualification_linked` | candidate/lifecycle/sample, Report revision, audit digest, adjudication, accounting summary digest | Compact reference; ticket-39 retains raw audit. |

The materialized `attempt_balance` stores reservation R, liability U, an ordered
set of unique provider charge lines and signed sum, coverage-completeness evidence,
estimate metadata, and `actual_state` of `unreported`, `partial`, `known`, `unknown`,
or `conflicted`. It is derived from immutable events and rebuilt on recovery.

A separate `experiment_cost_line` records applicable non-model support, human-review,
and venue costs with the same unique line identity, evidence/coverage state, and
experiment attribution. These never consume ADR 0013's model allocation, lifecycle, or
diagnostic limits, but do count in the aggregate user experiment liability below.

## Idempotency and reconciliation

A provider charge line is idempotent on
`(provider_account_pseudonym, provider_charge_line_identity)`. Exact replay returns the
existing line and writes no second charge. A changed amount, attempt, occurrence time, or
digest preserves both inputs, creates conflict, and sets `provider_receipt_conflict`.
Several distinct unique lines may link to one attempt; this supports a Run that makes
several billable model requests. A charge line linked to several attempts, an aggregate
invoice that cannot be allocated, or candidate/auth/venue conflict is unmatched and held.
A documented provider credit is a separate unique line linked to the adjusted charge;
history is never overwritten.

Let R be the accepted $3 reservation and U be the documented defensible maximum total
liability for the attempt, established by authorized preflight for the selected model,
request limits, and provider/client guard. U must be an exact positive integer and
U >= R. An absent, stale, unsupported, or non-enforceable U makes model admission
indeterminate: a $3 reservation alone does not bound a potentially higher provider charge.

For each attempt exposure is exactly one of:

- U while no final provider coverage exists, including unreported or partial charge lines;
- the signed sum of all unique provider lines only after an explicit authenticated
  `provider_coverage_completed` record covers the attempt's whole charge period; or
- **indeterminate** when coverage, U, billing lag, or attribution is unknown/conflicted.

Final actual sum replaces U in model weekly/allocation totals; individual lines and
reservation are never added again. Store `actual_sum_minus_reservation` and
`actual_sum_minus_liability`. A final sum above U or an applicable allocation/weekly
ceiling holds dispatch. An actual above R but within U and every applicable ceiling
settles normally: R is not a maximum charge. Zero/lower final actual does not erase
counters. Estimates display beside reconciliation but cannot
settle, credit, or enlarge budget.

For an applicable week, `week_liability` sums final actual for completed attempts and U
for each incomplete attempt. `experiment_liability` similarly sums every applicable
model, support, human-review, and venue cost line/commitment across all experiment weeks.
A non-model cost must have a documented U and final-coverage rule; otherwise it is
indeterminate. Availability exists only with no indeterminate exposure and no billing-lag,
integrity, capacity, or reconciliation hold:

    week_liability + new_attempt_U <= weekly_model_envelope
    experiment_liability + new_attempt_U < experiment_cap_micros
    allocation_liability + new_attempt_U <= allocation_ceiling
    lifecycle_spend + lifecycle_spend_increment <= lifecycle_spend_ceiling
    diagnostic_spend + diagnostic_spend_increment <= diagnostic_spend_ceiling
    lifecycle_attempts + 1 <= lifecycle_attempt_ceiling
    diagnostic_attempts + diagnostic_increment <= diagnostic_attempt_ceiling

The strict second comparison provides the requested aggregate user experiment cap below
$50: `experiment_cap_micros = 50_000_000`. It is a lifetime experiment guard across
applicable model, support, review, and venue cost, in addition to ADR 0013's $150
model-only weekly envelope. This proposal does not claim historical spend or amend ADR
0013. Any unknown prior actual or liability in the experiment population makes
availability indeterminate and blocks new experiment admission.

Reservation week is model-budget attribution even when actual arrives after rollover;
source occurrence/billing period is retained separately. Rollover makes a configured new
week but cannot release old reservations, clear holds, or erase the experiment hold.

### Dual attribution

| Attempt kind | Weekly charge | Lifecycle spend/count | Diagnostic spend/count |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Initial rehearsal/presentation Run | once | its lifecycle U/final actual, +1 | none |
| Authorized retry | once | parent lifecycle U/final actual, +1 | diagnostics U/final actual, +1 |
| Guard, transport, billing diagnostic | once | none | diagnostics U/final actual, +1 |

Weekly liability contains each charge once. For allocation ceilings, a retry's same U or
final actual is attributed to both its parent lifecycle $30 spend ceiling and diagnostics
$30 spend ceiling, while still appearing once in the $150 weekly total. A retry cannot
switch models, become initial, or convert a failed/uncertain sample into a clean one.

## Holds, capacity, retention, and restart

These reasons block **new model launch**: `billing_unknown`, `billing_lag`,
`launch_or_dispatch_unknown`, `provider_receipt_conflict`,
`unmatched_provider_charge`, `above_cap_actual`, `ledger_missing_or_corrupt`,
`recovery_incomplete`, `capacity_exhausted`, `forwarder_control_unreconciled`,
`venue_or_mediated_readiness_failed`, `history_unavailable`, and every ticket-37
execution/effect recovery hold.

A budget hold suppresses model launch, not admission. Continue bounded Notification
admission/coalescing, preserve source records and unresolved effects, and reconcile
them when no paid Run starts. Hold never drops pending Notifications, resets a week, or
replays a confirmed effect. Revocation/cancellation cannot undo a dispatched request.

Proposed capacity: 512 active/unsettled attempts and 8,192 accounting events, with four
future-event slots (launch, dispatch, terminal, reconciliation) held at each reservation.
Check that headroom inside reservation transaction. Exhaustion retains records and holds;
it never silently evicts or compacts unresolved state.

Keep full sanitized accounting events, reservations, holds, and reconciliation for 52
completed weeks. Cap retained idempotency tombstones at 65,536 entries or 16 MiB,
whichever comes first, and account for them in the 8,192-event/active-store capacity
budget. Before either retention/cap reaches its boundary, an operator must create a
read-back-verified archival handoff containing all charge-line identities, attempt/
reservation identities, coverage states, and holds, then atomically register its digest
and active duplicate-rejection index. If that handoff is missing, unreadable, conflicted,
or cannot answer a duplicate check, set `history_unavailable` and hold dispatch; never
silently discard a tombstone. Retain compact week/lifetime summaries only while their
bounded archive/index provenance remains readable. This differs from ADR 0010's 24-hour
telemetry and ADR 0014/ticket-39's raw-audit 30-day maximum. Audit expiry does not erase
accounting or make a historical sample clean.

Operator-only control may inspect a sanitized ledger, attach an authoritative billing
record, record provider adjustment, mark unmatched billing, dispose an irrecoverable
attempt, or clear one reconciled hold. Each is immutable with pseudonymized operator and
rationale reference. No action changes history, invents actuals, releases unreconciled
reservation, resets week, or auto-retries. Missing/corrupt/stale/untrusted storage holds
dispatch until authoritative reconstruction creates a new generation carrying all known
charges, reservations, holds, and tombstones.

## Ticket-37 / 36 interface

This is proposed shared vocabulary, not current implementation.

| Operation | Preconditions | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `reserve_for_attempt` | Receiver has lifecycle/candidate context; no caller ID. | Committed attempt/reservation, week, accounting receipt. |
| `register_lease` | Ticket 36 receives committed attempt, Run, service `anthropic`, scope, expiry. | Sanitized Forwarder receipt; ambiguity holds reservation and prevents launch. |
| `claim_launch` | Matching reservation and lease receipt. | Journal/accounting launch claim commits together; duplicate starts nothing. |
| `report_dispatch` | Trusted observation keyed by attempt. | Records failed-before-dispatch, dispatched-unknown, partial, or justified no-op. Uncertain dispatch holds; no replay. |
| `report_terminal` | Ticket-37 derived result keyed by attempt. | Evidence only; missing usage unknown and success does not settle cost. |
| `reconcile_provider_cost` | Authorized importer gives unique charge lines, coverage-completion evidence, and sanitized metadata. | Idempotently records lines/unknown and recomputes final sum or liability. |
| `retry_after_reconcile` | Effects reconciled/disposed; accounting capacity/no hold. | Fresh attempt/reservation/Run/lease; dual attribution. |

A Forwarder receipt is only future mediation/dispatch correlation, not billing evidence.
Accounting never blocks ticket-37 effect reconciliation; ticket 37 never uses budget
hold to discard recovery work.

## Qualification and acceptance

A comparison set pins candidate/model, intended API auth, intended venue, rubric version,
allocation/week, Fault revision, and sample matrix before evaluation. It has two
primary-presentation Faults and one designated fallback, recording cold/Memory condition
with at least one of each. A claimed presentation scope requires a primary sample there.
Fallback remains labelled fallback.

A sample is clean only if all are true:

1. Reviewed Fault definition/Ground truth predates it and intended-auth/venue evidence is
   present.
2. Each lifecycle contains and independently links its initial Report, supported
   match/update, and final resolution. Ticket 37 records their complete execution/effect
   path within 300 seconds; uncertain effect is reconciled and confirmed work is not
   replayed.
3. Accounting has reservation R, defensible U, complete provider coverage and
   reconciled sum of every charge line, correct dual spend/count attribution, and no
   billing hold. Failures/retries/estimates/deltas remain visible.
4. Ticket 39/ADR 0014 has complete audit, Report/evidence identity, supported correct
   final Mechanism and arithmetic, human adjudication, and no dispute, missing/
   unverifiable/expired-before-review evidence, or corrected-defect condition.
5. Ticket 41 has no unresolved Change stage, untracked intervention, or material
   retrieval/application gap.

Any failed, uncertain, unverifiable, disputed, or billing-unreconciled sample prevents
the set qualifying. Replacement consumes the allocation in that or later approved week
and leaves the failure visible. Least expensive compares only candidates with comparable
complete reconciled evidence; it is not a global-cost claim.

| Evidence class | Future acceptance cases | Not established now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source/static | schema/amount validation, strict below-$50, idempotency, fixed weeks, redaction | billing identity/coverage, storage durability, model behavior |
| Offline Receiver/Forwarder seam | concurrent reserve, multi-line charge/coverage replay and late line, every crash window, restart, capacity/archive handoff, unknown/above liability, retry, no replay effect | native client, billing, tenant/direct route, upstream effect |
| Offline audit/qualification seam | matrix, cold/Memory, grade/rollup, revised/disputed/expired evidence, Change gaps | human diagnosis, real Fault, venue/model quality |
| Authorized provider preflight | current rates/limits, billing lag/coverage, client guard, mediated endpoint/trust | purchase, paid sample, auth change, qualification |
| Authorized venue/model | real auth/venue/Forwarder, billing reconciliation, audit/adjudication, effects/time | reliability/cost guarantee beyond evidence |

## Open prerequisites

Paid admission remains closed pending: an authorized billing record shape with stable
per-charge identity, account scope/retention/adjustment semantics and measured lag;
current rates/limits and defensible total-liability/client guard; ticket-36
client/streaming/direct-route/control policy;
ticket-37 journal/receipt schema; ticket-39 audit grade/rollup; ticket-41 Change/retrieval
links; production storage/identity/backup/reconstruction acceptance; approved
candidate/allocation list; and representative Fault/venue samples.

No prerequisite is inferred from source, synthetic ledger, local TLS fixture, or
historical measurement. The interim state is held model admission and labelled replay.
