## Required corrections

1. **Stored week shape permits invalid attribution (plan lines 46-56).** Requiring a Monday key, positive 167/168/169-hour interval and the start UTC date equal to the key still admits Monday noon, nonzero seconds, or an interval ending on Tuesday. These are not Monday-midnight New York weeks and can misattribute history into allocation buckets. Preserve the no-recomputation rule, but define the conservative stored representation exactly: exact `date` key (not datetime/subclass), exact UTC datetimes; start/end at hour 04 or 05 with zero minutes/seconds/microseconds; dates respectively key and key+7; permitted duration; same-key identical boundaries; no overlap; reserved_at membership. Require fresh current week agreement. This is structural validation of hypothetical stored input, not historical timezone authenticity; the later adapter must establish provenance. Pin malformed old weeks, not only current ones, in tests.

2. **Retry continuation and the no-relabel claim are underdefined (lines 84-95).** Several ordinary initial Runs are allowed in a lifecycle, but “current chain tip” is not defined when there are several initial roots. A1, A2, retry(A1) and then retry(A2) has one successor per predecessor yet conflicts with one interpretation of the tip rule. Select one exact conservative rule; the simplest subset requires a retry predecessor to be the latest chronological attempt in that lifecycle, preserving same profile/week and reconciliation. Define candidate ordering explicitly (`at >` every historical reserved_at, if candidate is governed by strict history ordering). More fundamentally, the current input has no terminal outcome: it cannot distinguish two legitimate ordinary initial Runs from a failed initial relabeled as another initial before any retry. Either add a small closed hypothetical outcome/continuation fact and derive ordinary-versus-retry eligibility from it, refusing unknown, or narrow the no-relabel claim to validation of declared structural lineage and expressly defer semantic classification to the future trusted adapter. Do not imply that the current fields prove failed work was not relabeled. Test multiple-root predecessor choices and failed/unknown ordinary continuation consistent with the selected rule. Effect reconciliation remains a hypothetical fact and never execution authority.

3. **Journal generation has no type/range contract (lines 102-116).** Equality alone would accept booleans or incompatible values, and the broad UUID paragraph could be misread to cover it. Pin exact `int` in `1..2**31-1`, matching the existing journal generation domain, for snapshot and candidate; compare only after exact validation. Explicitly distinguish UUID experiment/identity fields from numeric generation. Add bool, subclass, zero, negative, overflow and mismatch cases.
